JPGs

JPG images are good for photos with large color palettes, they do not take exorbitantly increase file size. On the other hand they do not allow for transparent pixels.

GIFs

GIFs are good for animations, but the trade off is that they're limited in color palette. Transparent pixels are a binary option of GIFs.

PNGs

PNG images are good for anything thats not a photo or animated. For photos a PNG file of the same quality would be bigger than an equivalent JPG. However, they deal with opacity just fine, and they dont have color palette limitations. Great for icons, technical diagrams, logos, etc.

SVGs

SVGs are vector-based graphics format, this means it can scale up or down to any dimension without loss of quality. Pretty much same use case as PNGs, use them whenever you can.
